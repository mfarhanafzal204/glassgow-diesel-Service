Our Repair Process

A transparent, step-by-step approach to guarantee quality.

1. Diagnosis & Inspection

We start with a comprehensive inspection and use advanced diagnostic tools to accurately identify the root cause of the issue.

2. Disassembly & Repair

Our expert technicians carefully disassemble the component, replacing faulty parts with high-quality OEM spares and performing precision repairs.

3. Testing & Calibration

After reassembly, the component undergoes rigorous testing and calibration on our state-of-the-art equipment to ensure it meets or exceeds manufacturer specifications.

4. Final Quality Check & Delivery

A final quality assurance check is performed before the part is ready for delivery, ensuring complete customer satisfaction.

Our Legacy

The Founder: Sardar Muhammad

Glassgow Diesel Service was established in the 1960s by our founder, Sardar Muhammad. The journey began at Glassgow Diesel Laboratory, 14 no. Chungi Multan, with a focus on repairing diesel pumps for all kinds of tractors. His vision was to create a service center built on precision, reliability, and customer trust.

As times evolved, so did we. Today, Glassgow Diesel Service and Turbo House stands at Shop 122H, General Bus Stand, Multan, offering a comprehensive suite of expanded services. We now provide expert fault tracing for EFI vehicles, injector testing, and complete servicing for trucks and all types of machinery diesel pumps and turbochargers, continuing the legacy of quality and trust that Sardar Muhammad established.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of vehicles do you service?

We service a wide range of vehicles, including tractors, trucks, buses, generators, excavators, and other heavy machinery. We specialize in both diesel pumps and electronic EFI systems for modern vehicles.

Do you offer warranties on your repairs?

Yes, all our repairs come with a service warranty. The duration and terms depend on the nature of the repair and the parts used. We stand by the quality of our work.

How long does a typical turbocharger repair take?

The turnaround time can vary depending on the extent of the damage and parts availability. However, we strive to complete most standard turbo repairs within 1-2 business days. We also offer new turbochargers and cartridges for faster replacement.
